Voyage takes care of two things for you:

1. **Asking permission to travel** (a *travel request*), and
2. **Getting your money back** for what you spent on work (an *expense report*).

The map above shows every step and who does it. Select any step to read how to do it.

## The short version

- **Before the trip:** fill in a travel request. Your manager (and sometimes others) approve it.
- **After the trip:** add your bills to an expense report and submit it. Your manager approves it, then Finance checks it and pays you.
- **The whole time:** you can see exactly where your request is, and you get a notification at every step.

> Tip: Not every expense needs a trip. Taxi rides to a client in your own city, or a team lunch, go straight into an expense report.
