Everything waiting for you is in **Approvals** (the menu shows a number when something is waiting). You also get a notification.

1. Open **Approvals**. The **Inbox** lists what is waiting, oldest first. Red means it is overdue.
2. Select a request. On the right you see the purpose, dates, amounts, receipts, any **Policy exception** messages with the employee's explanation, and who else approves it.
3. Choose:
   - **Approve**: it moves on to the next approver, or to Finance if you are the last one.
   - **Return**: send it back for changes. Say what needs fixing.
   - **Reject**: stop it. Say why.

A comment is required to return or reject, so the employee knows what to do.

## Approve several at once

Tick the boxes next to several requests and select **Approve all**. Check each one first: you are approving all of them.

## Good to know

- You cannot approve your own requests. They always go to someone else.
- If you don't act in time, the request moves up to your manager so the employee isn't stuck.
- **History** shows everything you have decided.

> Tip: Keyboard users: **J** and **K** move through the list; **A**, **R** and **X** approve, return and reject.
