Open **Travel** or **Expenses** from the menu and select any request. The progress bar at the top shows how far it has gone, and the **Approvals** panel shows every approver, who has acted, and who has it now.

## What the statuses mean

| Status | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | Saved, but not sent yet. | Finish it and submit. |
| Awaiting approval | With an approver now. The page shows who. | Nothing. You will be notified. |
| Returned | Someone needs a change. | Read the comment, fix it, send it again. See [Returned for changes](help:returned-request). |
| Approved | Your trip is approved. | Book your travel. |
| Finance review | Your expenses are approved and Finance is checking them. | Nothing. |
| Payment scheduled | Finance has set the payment date. | Nothing. |
| Paid | The money has been paid to you. | Check your bank account. |
| Rejected | Not approved. The comment says why. | Talk to the approver if it is unclear. |
| Withdrawn | You took it back yourself. | Start a new one if needed. |

> Tip: Changed your mind? While a request is **Awaiting approval** or **Returned**, select **Withdraw**. Withdrawing is final; to change something instead, ask the approver to return it to you.
