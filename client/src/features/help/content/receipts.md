## How to attach one

- **On a phone:** tap the receipt box and take a photo, or choose one from your gallery.
- **On a computer:** drag the file onto the box, paste a screenshot, or select the box to browse.
- Accepted files: **JPG, PNG, WEBP or PDF**, up to **10 MB** each. You can add several to one bill.

Make sure the photo shows the **amount, the date and the shop or company name**.

## Lost a receipt?

1. Add the expense anyway. It shows a red **Policy exception:** message saying it needs a receipt.
2. If you have any other proof, such as a payment screenshot from your bank or UPI app, attach that instead.
3. When you submit, Voyage asks **why**. Explain what happened, for example "the taxi driver had no receipt".
4. Your approver decides. Honest explanations are usually accepted for small amounts.

> Important: Claim each bill only once. Bills with the same date, amount and place as one you already claimed are flagged automatically.
