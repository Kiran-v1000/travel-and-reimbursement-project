Open **Audit trail**. Every important action is recorded: sign-ins, approvals, payments, policy and rule changes, exports, and refused access attempts.

- **Search** by person, action or record ID, or filter by record type.
- **Verify integrity** checks that no entry has been changed or removed. Each entry is chained to the one before, so any edit breaks the chain and shows up here.
- **Export CSV** downloads the entries as a spreadsheet. Exports are themselves recorded.

> Note: Auditors can read everything but can't change anything.
