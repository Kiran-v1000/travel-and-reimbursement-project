Go to **Administration**.

## Approval routing

Rules decide who approves a request, based on the amount, the trip type, the department or cost center, and whether it breaks policy.

1. Use **Test the routing** first: pick an employee and an amount to see exactly who would approve it.
2. Select **New rule** or **Edit** to change who approves. Each level can be the manager, the skip-level manager, the department head, the top of the reporting line, a role such as Finance, or a named person.
3. Every change creates a new version. Requests already submitted keep the rules they started with.

## Policy & categories

Set the lead times, hotel and meal limits, claim deadline and retention period. For each expense category, set the per-bill limit, when a receipt is needed, and its accounting ledger. These numbers also appear automatically in this user guide.

## Organisation & currencies

Departments, cost centers and exchange rates.

> Note: Every change is recorded in the audit trail with the old and new values.
