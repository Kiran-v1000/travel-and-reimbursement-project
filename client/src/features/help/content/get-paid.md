After your manager (and anyone else needed) approves your expense report:

1. **Finance review:** Finance checks the bills and the coding. If something is missing, they return it to you with a comment.
2. **Payment scheduled:** Finance sets a payment date and method (bank transfer, with payroll, UPI or cheque). You get a notification.
3. **Paid:** Finance records the payment with a transaction reference. You get a notification, and the report shows **Paid** with the date and reference.

You can always see the amount still due on the home page under **Awaiting reimbursement**.

> Tip: The transaction reference (UTR) shown on the report is what your bank shows too. Use it if you need to check with your bank.

> Note: If the amount paid is different from what you claimed, Finance must record a reason, and you can see it on the report.
