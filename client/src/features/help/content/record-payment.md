After the money has actually been sent:

1. In **Finance**, open the **Scheduled** tab and select the report.
2. Select **Record payment**.
3. Check the **Method** and **Amount**, choose the date it was **Paid on**, and enter the **Transaction reference** (the UTR or cheque number).
4. Select **Mark as paid**. The employee is notified with the reference.

If you pay a different amount than was approved (for example an advance was deducted), you must give a reason. It is recorded and shown on the report.

> Tip: Clicking twice is safe: the same reference is only recorded once.
