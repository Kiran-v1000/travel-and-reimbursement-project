> You'll need: your company email address. Nothing else to install.

## Sign in

1. Open Voyage in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox, on a computer or a phone).
2. Select **Sign in with Microsoft** and use your normal company account, the same one you use for email.
3. If your company gave you a password instead, enter your **Work email** and **Password** and select **Sign in**.

The first time you sign in with a password, you are asked to choose your own password.

## The home page

After signing in you land on **Home**. It shows, at a glance:

- **Awaiting reimbursement**: money you have claimed that is not paid yet.
- **Reimbursed this year**: what has already been paid back to you.
- **Active trips**: your upcoming and pending trips.
- **Needs your attention**: anything you should act on, such as a draft to finish or a request returned for changes.

The two big buttons, **Request travel** and **New expense**, start the two things you will do most.

## Moving around

- On a computer, the menu is on the left: **Home**, **Travel**, **Expenses**, **Approvals** and more.
- On a phone, tap the **menu** icon in the top-left corner.
- The **?** button at the top always brings you back to this help.

> Tip: You only see the menu items for your role. If you approve other people's requests, you also see **Approvals**.
