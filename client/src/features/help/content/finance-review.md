Reports arrive in **Finance** once every approver has signed off.

1. Open **Finance**. The **Review queue** lists reports waiting for you, oldest first. Tick **Only reports with policy exceptions** to see those first.
2. Open a report. Check the receipts, the amounts and any **Policy exception** messages with the employee's explanation.
3. If the cost center, project or a category is wrong, select **Recode**, correct it and select **Save coding**. Every change is recorded.
4. Then choose:
   - **Approve**: choose the payment method and the date it will be paid (**Scheduled for**), then select **Schedule**. If the report breaks policy, you must give a reason for approving it anyway.
   - **Return**: send it back to the employee with what to fix.
   - **Reject**: stop it, with the reason.

> Important: You cannot review, recode or pay your own claim. Another Finance colleague has to.

Scheduled reports move to the **Scheduled** tab until they are paid. See [Record a payment](help:record-payment).
