> You'll need: your bills or receipts (photos are fine) and the dates you paid.

## 1. Start a report

1. Select **New expense** on the home page (or **New**, then **Expense report**).
2. Choose **Link a trip** if the costs belong to an approved trip, or **No trip** for local or general business costs.
3. Give it a **Report title** (for example "Client visit - Pune, March") and a **Business purpose**.
4. Choose the **Cost center**, add a **Project code** if you have one, and select **Continue to add expenses**.

## 2. Add each bill

1. Select **Add expense** (or **Add first expense**).
2. Fill in the **Date**, **Category** (taxi, meals, hotel and so on), **Merchant** (who you paid), **Amount** and **Currency**.
3. Say how you paid under **Paid with**: personal card, cash, UPI or corporate card.
4. Add the receipt: drag the photo or PDF onto the box, paste it, or select it from your phone or computer. See [Adding receipts](help:receipts).
5. Repeat for every bill. The total updates as you go.

## 3. Send it

1. Check the **Policy** panel. **Within policy** means everything follows the rules. If anything is flagged, see [What the coloured messages mean](help:policy-badges).
2. Select **Submit report**. If something is red, you are asked to explain why first.

Your report goes to your manager, then to Finance, who schedules your payment. See [When you get paid](help:get-paid).

> Tip: Claim within {{submissionDeadlineDays}} days of spending. Older bills are flagged as late.

> Tip: Not finished? Your report is saved as a **Draft** automatically. Come back to it any time from **Expenses**.
