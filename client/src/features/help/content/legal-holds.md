Open **Retention & holds**.

## Retention

Closed records are kept for the retention period set in policy. After that, their receipts and personal details are removed, while codes, dates and amounts are kept for the books. The page shows how many records are due, and nothing is removed until an administrator runs it or switches on the nightly run.

## Legal holds

A legal hold protects records, for example for a tax audit or a dispute. Held records are never removed and can't be changed or deleted.

1. Select **Place legal hold**.
2. Choose what to hold: an employee, one expense report, one trip, or everything created in a date range.
3. Give the reason (and a case reference if you have one), then select **Place hold**.

To end it, select **Release** and give the reason. Both actions are recorded.
