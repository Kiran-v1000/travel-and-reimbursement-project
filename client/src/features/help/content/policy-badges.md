Voyage checks every trip and bill against the company rules **as you type**, so you can fix things before anyone else sees them. You see one of three kinds of message:

- **Within policy** (green): everything follows the rules.
- **Heads up:** (yellow): worth a second look, but it does not block anything.
- **Policy exception:** (red): a rule is crossed. You can still submit, but you must explain why.

## The messages, in plain words

| The message says | It means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| …needs a receipt | This bill needs a receipt. | Attach one, or explain why you don't have it. |
| …above the (category) limit | The bill is more than the category allows. | Explain why, or claim only the allowed amount. |
| …days old; claims must… | The bill is older than {{submissionDeadlineDays}} days. | Explain the delay. |
| …dated in the future | The date is after today. | Fix the date. |
| …looks like a duplicate | A very similar bill (same date, amount and place) was already claimed. | Check you haven't claimed it twice. |
| …falls outside the trip dates | The bill is before or after the linked trip. | Check the date, or explain. |
| …exceeds the approved estimate | The total is more than {{estimateTolerancePct}}% above the trip estimate. | Explain the difference. |
| Submitted … day(s) before travel | The trip was requested late (the rule is {{leadDaysDomestic}} days ahead for domestic trips). | Explain the urgency. |
| Lodging / Meals average … | Hotel above {{lodgingCapDomestic}} a night or meals above {{mealsPerDiem}} a day (domestic). | Explain, or lower the amount. |

## How to explain an exception

When anything is red and you submit, Voyage asks you **why** in one box. Write one or two clear sentences, for example:

> Example: "Only hotel available near the client site during the trade fair; the client confirmed the dates late."

Your explanation goes to every approver and stays with the request.
