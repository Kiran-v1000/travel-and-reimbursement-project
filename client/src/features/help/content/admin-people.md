Go to **Administration**, then **People**.

## One person

Select **Add person**, fill in their name, work email, employee ID, department, cost center, manager and role, then save. To change someone, select them in the list.

## Many people at once (from HR)

1. Select **Import CSV**, then download the template.
2. Fill it in from your HR system: one row per employee with their manager's email.
3. Upload it. Voyage **checks the whole file first** and lists every problem, such as a missing manager or a reporting loop, without changing anything.
4. When the check is clean, select **Import**.

## Leavers

Switch the person to inactive. They lose access straight away and all their sessions end. Their history is kept.

> Important: People who sign in with Microsoft are added automatically the first time they sign in.
