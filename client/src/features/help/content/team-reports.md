- **Travel** and **Expenses** lists: switch from **Mine** to **My team** to see requests from everyone below you.
- **Reports** shows spending by month, category, department and cost center, plus open approvals and policy exceptions. Choose **Year to date**, **Last 90 days** or **Last 30 days** at the top.
- Use **Export** on a list, or **CSV** on a report panel, to download it as a spreadsheet.

The figures are refreshed every few minutes, and the page shows when they were last updated.
