Select your name in the top-right corner, then **Profile & delegation**.

## Your details

Your email, employee ID, department, cost center, location and **Reports to** (your manager) come from the company's records. If something is wrong, for example the wrong manager, contact {{helpContact}}. Your requests go to the manager shown here.

## Change your password

Only shown if you sign in with a password (not Microsoft).

1. Select **Change password**.
2. Enter your current password, then a new one: at least 12 characters with upper and lower case letters, a number and a symbol.
3. Every other device you were signed in on is signed out, for your safety.

## Signed-in devices

**Signed-in devices** lists everywhere you are signed in. If you see a device you don't recognise, select **Sign out other devices** and change your password.

> Note: For your security, Voyage signs you out after a while without activity, sooner for Finance and administrator accounts. Just sign in again.
