> You'll need: where and when you are going, why, and a rough idea of the costs.

1. Select **Request travel** on the home page (or **New**, then **Travel request**).
2. **Trip details:** choose **Domestic** or **International**, give the trip a short **Trip title** (for example "Customer workshop in Pune") and explain the **Business purpose**: who you are meeting and what it will achieve.
3. **Route & dates:** enter **From** and **To** (separate several cities with commas), the **Departure** and **Return** dates, and anyone travelling with you under **Travelers**.
4. **Estimated costs:** enter rough amounts for transport, lodging, meals and other costs. **Use policy rates** fills meals and hotel from the company limits for you. Rough numbers are fine; you claim the real amounts later.
5. Choose the **Cost center** (who pays for the trip) and a **Project code** if you have one.
6. Look at the **Policy check**. If something is outside the rules, it tells you what and asks **Why is this exception needed?** Write one or two clear sentences.
7. Select **Submit for approval**. Not ready yet? Select **Save as draft** and finish later.

## What happens next

Your request goes to your manager first. Larger or international trips also go to more people, for example your department head or Finance. You get a notification when it is approved, returned or rejected. See [Track your request](help:track-request).

> Tip: A clear business purpose is the number one thing that gets trips approved quickly.

> Important: Book your tickets only after the trip shows **Approved**.
