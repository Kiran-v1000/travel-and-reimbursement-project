## What if I lost a receipt?

Add the expense anyway and attach any other proof you have, such as a bank or UPI screenshot. When you submit, explain what happened. See [Adding receipts](help:receipts).

## Who approves my request, and how long does it take?

Your manager always approves first. Bigger, international or out-of-policy requests also go to others, for example your department head or Finance. Open the request to see every approver and who has it now. If an approver doesn't act in time, it moves up to their manager automatically.

## Can I edit a request after submitting it?

Not while it is with an approver. Either ask the approver to **Return** it to you (then you can edit it and send it again), or select **Withdraw** and start a new one. Withdrawing is final. For trips, **Duplicate** copies all the details into a new request for you.

## I claimed the wrong amount. What do I do?

If it is still **Awaiting approval**, ask your approver to return it so you can fix it. If it is already with Finance, contact {{helpContact}}.

## Do I need a trip to claim expenses?

No. Choose **No trip** for local costs such as a taxi to a client or a working lunch.

## My manager is wrong in Voyage.

Your manager comes from the company's records. Contact {{helpContact}} to have it corrected. Requests always follow the manager shown on your **Profile & delegation** page.

## Why was I signed out?

For your safety, Voyage signs you out after a period without activity, and when your password changes. Sign in again; your drafts are saved.

## When will I be paid?

Usually on the date Finance schedules after approving your report. You are notified when it is scheduled and when it is paid. See [When you get paid](help:get-paid).

## Still stuck?

Contact {{helpContact}}. Include the request code (for example EX-2026-0042) so they can find it quickly.
