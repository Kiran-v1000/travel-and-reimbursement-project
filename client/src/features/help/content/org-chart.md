1. Select **Org chart** in the menu. It opens on you: your managers above, your team below.
2. Select anyone to move to them, or use **Find someone** to search by name.
3. **Me** brings you back; **Top** jumps to the head of the company.

Each card shows the person's title, department and team size. It also says who approves that person's travel and expenses first.
