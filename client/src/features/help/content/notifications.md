- The **bell** at the top shows a red number when you have new notifications. Select it to see them, and select a notification to open the request it is about.
- You are notified when your request is approved, returned, rejected or paid, and (for approvers) when something is waiting for you.
- If your company has switched on email, you also get the important ones by email.

> Tip: Select **Mark all read** in the bell to clear the counter.
