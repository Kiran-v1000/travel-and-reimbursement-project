## What if I need to go over a limit?

You can still ask. Voyage shows the rule you are crossing and asks you to explain why. A short, honest reason, such as "the client asked for an urgent meeting tomorrow", is usually enough. Requests outside the rules may need one more approver.

> Note: Your manager and Finance see the same rules and your explanation, so there are no surprises.
