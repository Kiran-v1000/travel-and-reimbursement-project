1. Select your name in the top-right corner, then **Profile & delegation**.
2. Under **Approval delegation**, select **Delegate**.
3. Choose who to **Delegate to**, the dates (**From** and **Until**), and **What can they approve?** (travel and expenses, travel requests only, or expense reports only). Add a reason if you like.
4. Select **Create delegation**.

During those dates your approvals also appear in their **Approvals** inbox, marked as on your behalf. Every decision records both names.

To end it early, select **Revoke**.

> Note: Your delegate can't approve their own requests, even on your behalf.
